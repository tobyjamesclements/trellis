# ADR-005: Marks are derived, never stored as authority; keys, rubrics and policies are versioned and changes re-derive

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Storing marks makes every correction a migration and every replica a
potential conflict. Formative assessment corrects keys and rubrics often.

## Decision
- `mark = f(response, key_version, rubric_version, policy_version,
  engine_version)`; no table holds a mark that is not a stamped cache of
  `f` (DRV-01).
- Keys, rubrics and policies are content-hashed versions published as facts.
- A version change enqueues a partition recompute under a new generation;
  nothing is edited in place.
- The key-change policy (`best_of` default) is explicit and recorded in
  provenance.

## Consequences
- The engine must be deterministic across targets (DRV-03) and gated by
  golden vectors.
- Grade history is the fact log plus provenance, not a separate table.
- Interop consumers must accept that "final" grades can change (LTI and
  DIO Known Tensions).

## Alternatives rejected
- Store marks with a "needs regrade" flag: the flag is the migration; it is
  what Moodle does and what causes gradebook drift.
