# ADR-024: The NCSC Cyber Assessment Framework is the security assurance baseline

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The product owner requires that Trellis use and meet the NCSC Cyber
Assessment Framework (CAF). The CAF assesses an organisation's management of
cyber risk to the services it operates through four objectives (A: managing
security risk, B: protecting against cyber attack, C: detecting cyber
security events, D: minimising the impact of incidents), fourteen principles
and 39 contributing outcomes, each rated Achieved, Partially achieved or Not
achieved against indicators of good practice. Some contributing outcomes are
technical properties of the system; others are organisational processes.

## Decision
- The `security-and-assurance` capability (SEC) owns the technical controls
  and the evidence; `security-and-assurance/caf-mapping.md` maps every
  contributing outcome to the requirement that meets it, the organisational
  process that complements it, and the evidence produced.
- Target: every technical contributing outcome at **Achieved** before
  general availability; organisational outcomes are the operating
  organisation's responsibility and the tree names what the system supplies
  toward them.
- The mapping is written against the CAF's objective, principle and
  contributing-outcome structure (v3.2 numbering); change 014 re-baselines
  it against the latest published CAF version adopted by the assessing body
  before the first assessment.
- Security-relevant events (authentication, authorisation failures,
  administrative and privileged actions, key operations, data exports,
  operator access to tenant data) are logged in full to an immutable store
  with retention independent of the sampled application logs (this amends
  ADR-020's sampling rule).
- The NCSC Cloud Security Principles are used as the customer-facing
  statement of the service's security properties; the CAF is the assurance
  framework for the organisation operating it.

## Consequences
- A security logging pipeline, vulnerability management, an incident
  response plan and exercising become part of the initial build, not an
  afterthought (changes 001 and 014).
- Learner devices are untrusted and hold learning data offline (B3.d); the
  mapping states the accepted risk for learners and the encryption and
  purge rules for instructor devices.
- A monthly cost of roughly $60–80 per zone for CloudTrail, GuardDuty,
  Security Hub, Config, Inspector and immutable log storage joins the zone
  floor (SEC cost model).

## Alternatives rejected
- ISO 27001 or SOC 2 as the primary framework: both remain useful for
  customers; the owner named the CAF, and its contributing outcomes map
  cleanly onto the design.
- Cyber Essentials Plus only: a baseline, not an assessment of an operated
  service.
