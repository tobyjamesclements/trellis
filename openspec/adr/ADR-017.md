# ADR-017: Content-addressed blobs in S3 with bidirectional cross-region replication and CloudFront origin-group failover

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Content, submissions and large fact bodies must be available in every region
of a zone without a coordination step, and must be immutable to fit the
fact model.

## Decision
- Every blob is stored at `content/<sha256>` (course content) or
  `subjects/<tenant>/<subject>/<sha256>` (submissions). Uploads use presigned
  PUTs with `x-amz-checksum-sha256`, so S3 rejects bytes that do not hash to
  the key.
- Each region of a zone has a bucket; replication rules run in both
  directions. Facts referencing a blob carry its `origin_region`.
- CloudFront distributions use an origin group (regional bucket, peer bucket)
  with failover on 403/404/5xx, so a not-yet-replicated blob is served from
  its origin.
- Deletion happens only for erasure, takedown and retention (terminal
  operations), applied in every bucket.

## Consequences
- Duplicate content across courses is stored once.
- A blob referenced by a fact may be missing for seconds; readers show
  `blob_pending`.
- Video is not hosted; external players via LTI or URL.

## Alternatives rejected
- Single bucket with multi-region access points: MRAP supports failover but
  replication is still asynchronous; origin groups give the same result with
  simpler IAM.
