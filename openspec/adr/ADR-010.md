# ADR-010: Version vectors over dense per-stream sequences with contiguity high-water marks and abandonment

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Freshness of a view must be provable, cross-region comparable and usable as
a cursor. Region-local arrival orders are not comparable across regions;
timestamps are not proofs.

## Decision
- The writer of record is the device. Each `(writer_device, subject, scope)`
  stream has a dense `seq` allocated durably on the device (FLS-03) and a
  hash chain (`prev`).
- A view's vector maps streams in its partition to the highest contiguous
  seq folded (MVA-03). Dominance is component-wise; the difference is the
  exact set of facts to fold.
- Rosters track HWM and pending seqs per stream; the writer fills gaps
  with `noop` facts; a 30-day unresolved gap is abandoned by a system fact
  so views can proceed while recording the hole (FLS-09).
- Sequences are per scope so that per-partition contiguity holds.

## Consequences
- Vector size is bounded by cohort size × devices; the header holds a
  digest and per-learner rows hold their own cursors.
- Instructor writers keep a sequence per `(subject, scope)` they write
  about.
- A device that loses its local log must re-register as a new device; its
  old stream is eventually abandoned.

## Alternatives rejected
- Per-region arrival sequences as the vector: not comparable across
  regions; kept only as an implementation detail for OneRoster deltas.
- Lamport/HLC-only freshness: a max timestamp does not prove inclusion of
  late-replicated facts.
