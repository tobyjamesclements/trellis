# ADR-019: Cohort size is bounded at 2,000 learners; larger enrolments are split into sub-cohorts

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The view partition is `(module, cohort)`. Its header and Merkle root receive
one write per fold batch; per-partition throughput and item-collection size
must stay bounded. Version vectors scale with members × devices.

## Decision
- A cohort holds at most 2,000 learners. Enrolment places learners into the
  smallest open sub-cohort and creates a new one when none has room
  (IDE-07). Concurrent creation in two regions is allowed and reconciled.
- Course-level views fold across sub-cohorts (MVA-06).
- Instructors see sub-cohorts as sections and may re-cohort by facts.

## Consequences
- A "class" of 5,000 is three partitions; rank and percentile across the
  whole class are computed at course level from sub-cohort rows.
- The bound is a tenant policy with a hard ceiling of 2,000.

## Alternatives rejected
- Unbounded cohorts with sharded headers: complex read-time folds for every
  view; hot partitions at deadlines.
