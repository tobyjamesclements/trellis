# ADR-006: Marking happens on the learner's device; answer-key exposure is accepted and controlled by visibility policy

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Immediate feedback offline requires the device to mark, which requires the
key on the device. Devices are untrusted. Trellis is formative-only.

## Decision
- The engine marks on device with the item's response processing and key.
- Facts carry the response, not the mark; the server re-derives (DRV-14)
  and treats the client mark as advisory telemetry.
- Key exposure is a known, accepted trade. Items declare a visibility
  policy (CAC-09): `immediate` (default), `after_submit` (soft, bypassable),
  `time_locked` (escrowed release key, a real barrier until reveal),
  `server_only` (key never leaves the server; deferred feedback).
- No proctoring, lockdown or anti-tamper features are built.

## Consequences
- Instructors must be told which policies are real barriers.
- `time_locked` items cannot be marked offline before reveal; `server_only`
  items cannot be marked offline at all.
- Item statistics (facility, discrimination) are computed from server
  derivations, not client marks.

## Alternatives rejected
- Homomorphic or zero-knowledge marking: research-grade, not deliverable.
- Server-only marking by default: kills offline feedback, the product's
  core.
