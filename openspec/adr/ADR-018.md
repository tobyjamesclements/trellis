# ADR-018: Interop posture: inbound payloads become facts; outbound payloads are projections from the home region with monotone timestamps and an epoch

## Status
Accepted

## Context
1EdTech services (AGS, OneRoster, Caliper, NRPS, Edu-API) assume a single
authoritative system with monotone modification times and settled values.
Trellis has neither globally.

## Decision
- Inbound: every received record or event is a fact; derivation decides its
  effect.
- Outbound writes: home region only, stability window, per-entity monotone
  timestamp continued from the sent ledger (`max(last + 1 ms, now)`),
  deterministic ids, ledger entry.
- Outbound cursor-based reads (OneRoster/Edu-API deltas, NRPS differences):
  served from the home region; modification times are region-relative; a
  tenant `epoch` in the registry is bumped on failover; delta requests with
  a stale epoch get `409 epoch_changed` and consumers full-sync.
- Every projected value carries the vector digest it was derived at.

## Consequences
- External gradebooks are delayed, eventually consistent copies; this is
  said in documentation and in the UI.
- SIS locks and platform rejections surface as reconciliation items for
  humans (DIO-17, LTI-19).
- Failover forces a full resync for delta consumers.

## Alternatives rejected
- Serving deltas from any region with `sync_hlc` as modification time: a
  late-replicated fact carries an old timestamp and is missed.
- Refusing to write back at all: cleaner, unusable for most platforms;
  offered as an option.
