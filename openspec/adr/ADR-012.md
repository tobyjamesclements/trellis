# ADR-012: Non-monotone derivations are recomputed over the union; side effects sit behind stability windows and are emitted only by the home region with a sent ledger

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Rank, percentile, at-risk and mastery cannot be merged across replicas.
Emails, score posts, credentials and SIS pushes cannot be un-sent. Both
regions see every fact (ADR-004), so both would fire.

## Decision
- Non-monotone results are NMRows computed wholesale at a vector and never
  merged (MVA-07).
- A side effect from a non-monotone predicate is an intent with
  `decided_at_vector`, fired only after window `W` if the predicate still
  holds at a dominating vector with no new facts for the subject (MVA-08).
  Defaults: notifications 5 min, AGS 60 s, credentials 24 h; never zero.
- All external side effects are emitted only by the tenant's home region,
  keyed by an idempotency key, and recorded in a region-owned sent ledger in
  the global table (readable everywhere).
- Home-region failover is a registry flip with an epoch bump; the new home
  region consults all ledgers before emitting backlog. The double-emission
  window (replication lag + config cache TTL, target ≤ 90 s) is accepted.

## Consequences
- Legitimate effects are delayed by `W`.
- Emission is unavailable while the home region is down and unflipped.
- Consumers must tolerate duplicates with identical ids.

## Alternatives rejected
- Emit from any region with a global ledger check: check-then-act race
  across regions without conditional writes.
- Emit from both and dedupe downstream: not possible for email.
