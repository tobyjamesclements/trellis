# ADR-020: Cost governance: on-demand everything, no provisioned concurrency, sampled logs, per-tenant budgets that never throttle learner sync

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The product promise is massive scale at very low cost with idle costing
nothing. The main hidden costs on Lambda architectures are provisioned
capacity, logs, and per-resource floors.

## Decision
- DynamoDB on-demand; no provisioned capacity; no DAX.
- No Lambda provisioned concurrency; SnapStart and Rust instead (ADR-001).
- Structured logs sampled 10% on success paths, 100% on errors; EMF metrics
  with bounded dimensions; X-Ray sampling 1%.
- Cost allocation tags per tenant; monthly "cost per active learner"
  rollup; budgets alert at 80% and throttle non-essential jobs (analytics
  rollups, digests, anti-entropy frequency) at 100%; learner sync, marking,
  and view folds are never throttled by budget.
- Fixed floor per zone (Route 53, KMS keys, health checks, dashboards) is
  documented and kept under ~$60/month.

## Consequences
- Bursts from idle can throttle on-demand tables briefly; the sync path
  queues rather than fails (FLS-13).
- Debugging relies on sampled logs plus fact provenance, not full traces.

## Alternatives rejected
- Provisioned capacity with autoscaling for deadline weeks: pays for idle.
