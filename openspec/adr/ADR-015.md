# ADR-015: Identity: no passwords; OIDC federation and magic links natively; Cognito only as a SAML bridge; uniqueness delegated; deterministic principal ids for federated identities; account merge

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Institutions bring their own identity providers. Passwords are a liability.
Uniqueness of usernames and emails cannot be enforced across regions.
Cognito user pools are single-region.

## Decision
- No password storage. Authentication is OIDC (native RP in every region),
  magic link (native, any region), LTI launch, or SAML through a Cognito
  pool in the tenant's home region.
- Principals for federated identities have deterministic ids
  (`usr_` + hash(issuer, sub)); SIS-sourced learners likewise from
  `(sis:<tenant>, sourcedId)`; email-only sign-ups mint ULIDs and may
  duplicate, resolved by `merge.v1` (IDE-11).
- Sessions and refresh tokens are stateless JWTs signed with regional keys
  registered as facts; revocation is a fact with replication-lag effect.

## Consequences
- SAML login is unavailable during a home-region outage (documented; per-
  region pools offered on request).
- Authorisation is eventually consistent (IDE-09).
- Magic-link emails are the one side effect any region may send (each
  request mints a distinct token; no dedupe concern).

## Alternatives rejected
- Cognito as the identity store: single-region, and its user attributes are
  mutable state outside the fact log.
- Native SAML SP on Lambda: XML signature handling risk; revisit later.
